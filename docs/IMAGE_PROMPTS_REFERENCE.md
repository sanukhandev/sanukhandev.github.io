## Visual direction and usage rules

- Brand: warm ivory `#E6D5BD`, soft black `#101010`, selective burnt orange `#D5360C`.
- Style: modern premium personal engineering portfolio, minimal 3D editorial imagery, restrained depth, soft side lighting, strong negative space; inspired by contemporary Framer portfolio composition, **not a copy of Rajib Kabir artwork**.
- All project visuals are **conceptual artwork**, **not** client screenshots or system diagrams representing exact implemented systems. Label them accordingly if published.
- Do not invent logos, UI screenshots, identifiable clients, app metrics, fabricated quotes, or third-party product branding.
- Avoid words baked into the generated image: render actual headings, project titles, and alt text as HTML.
- Export optimized AVIF/WebP and responsive sizes after reviewing; maintain native aspect ratio, clear crop, and quality. Keep image source/rights records.

### Common negative prompt (append to all)

"No visible text or pseudo-text, no watermarks, no logos, no brand names, no invented interface screens, no stock-photo clichés, no bright gradients, no neon cyberpunk, no blue-purple glow, no icons randomly floating around, no cartoon characters, no fake dashboards, no exaggerated particle effects, no overly glossy AI-generated look, no clutter. Keep subjects legible with deliberate negative space and realistic materiality."
